A speech-restoration research prototype combining frozen WavJEPA-Nat features, FiLM conditioning, and TEMF mean-flow generation.

The project explores conditioning a TEMF generator on representations from a frozen WavJEPA-Nat encoder. The repository includes the experimental implementation and CPU smoke tests.

[View the source code and research status](https://github.com/quangng2000/temf-wavjepa-film).
